The Quba Outreach Session was held on 27 July 2024, with a CMO day on 26 July. The session was in the Azerbaijani language. Its main aim was to promote EYP values such as diversity, intercultural learning and active citizenship among young people living in the regions of Azerbaijan.

The session was planned as a one-day event for local young people from Quba.
