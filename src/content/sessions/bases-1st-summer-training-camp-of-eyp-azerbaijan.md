BASES, the 1st Summer Training Camp of EYP Azerbaijan, was held on 22-25 August 2017 in Ilisu. It was an initiative to cultivate a culture of training in EYP Azerbaijan and was meant as an Alumni Development and Capacity Building event.

The camp aimed to bring together active alumni of EYP Azerbaijan and EYPers from across Europe, and to enhance their skills in three directions: chairing, organising and media. Its goal was to prepare more qualified officials for EYP events in Azerbaijan and abroad. It was open to both experienced participants and people with little EYP experience.
