The 10th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku from 10 to 14 July 2024, with 9 July as the CMOJ day. It was planned to gather more than 150 participants from Azerbaijan and across Europe, and the delegates chosen at the conference were to represent EYP Azerbaijan in the International Sessions of 2025.

Under the motto “Handmade in the Digital Age”, the conference encouraged academic discussions and debates on keeping human creativity, empathy and critical thinking alive as Artificial Intelligence becomes part of daily life, and on preserving our humanity alongside technological advancement.
