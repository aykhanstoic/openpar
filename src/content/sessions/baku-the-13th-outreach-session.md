The 13th Outreach Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku on 6 and 7 December 2025, with 5 December as the CMO day.

Under the motto “From the City of Winds to New Ideas”, its main aim was to create a safe, engaging and inspiring environment for young people in Baku, encouraging them to explore new perspectives while embracing the dynamic spirit of the capital. The session's vision linked Baku's identity as the City of Winds with the ideas of the new generation. By opening space for dialogue, critical thinking and inclusivity, it aimed to expand EYP's outreach and introduce its values to new young voices.
