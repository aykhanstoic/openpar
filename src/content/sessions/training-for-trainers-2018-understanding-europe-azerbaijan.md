Training for Trainers 2018 - Understanding Europe Azerbaijan was held on 1-2 December 2018 in Baku. It is a training within EYP Azerbaijan that aims to develop young active citizens as trainers of Understanding Europe Crash Courses in the coming years, and to build their soft skills such as critical thinking, leadership and decision making.

Training for Trainers events are held once a year in Azerbaijan and mark the start of the new academic year of Crash Courses in schools and universities.
