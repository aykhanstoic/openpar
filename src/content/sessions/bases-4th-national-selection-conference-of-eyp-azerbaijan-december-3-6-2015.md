The 4th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan, BASES, was held on 3-6 December 2015 in Baku, the “City of Winds and Flame”. It was planned to gather more than 80 participants from different regions of Azerbaijan, who would take part in discussions in 8 committees, with an international team of officials and journalists from 11 countries.

The selected participants were to represent Azerbaijan at the 81st International Session of EYP in Dublin and the 82nd International Session of EYP in Rennes.
