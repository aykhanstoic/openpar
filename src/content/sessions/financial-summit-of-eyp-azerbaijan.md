The Financial Summit of EYP Azerbaijan was held from 15 to 18 January 2026 under the theme “Decisions Define Value”. It explored how economic and financial decisions shape value in modern societies, from historical monetary systems to today’s global markets, with a focus on the role of choice, responsibility and priorities in defining economic outcomes.

By addressing finance, economics and business, the summit aimed to invite delegates to reflect on who defines value, how resources are allocated, and how short-term decisions influence long-term stability and sustainability.
