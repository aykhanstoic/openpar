The Human Resources Department of EYP Azerbaijan presented a workshop series of small-scale capacity-building events to equip active members with essential skills and knowledge. The Application Writing module was held in Baku on 18 August 2026.

The module explained the selection procedure from the HR perspective and discussed the common mistakes and strengths of applications. It also included a selection panel simulation, in which participants experienced the selection process and analysed real past applications.
