Crescendo, the 2nd Digital Session of EYP Azerbaijan, was held digitally from 13 to 16 May 2021, with the Officials' Training on 12 May. The name refers to the peak of a gradual increase in the volume of a musical passage.

The session aimed to gather young people from across Europe to discuss recent topics that affect, and are affected by, youth, and to give participants a space to voice their opinions.
