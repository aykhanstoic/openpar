The 10th Ganja Outreach Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 7-8 September 2024 in Ganja. The session was in the Azerbaijani language. Under the theme “Shaping Our Future Together”, it aimed to give young people a platform to discuss globally relevant issues and to celebrate Ganja's cultural heritage and historical significance. It also aimed to encourage understanding and cooperation between nations, the exploration of innovative solutions and civic engagement.

The programme was planned to open on 6 September at the NAYORA Resource Center in Baku, with the remaining days at ADAU in Ganja.
