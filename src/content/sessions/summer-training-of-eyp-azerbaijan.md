The Summer Training of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 25 and 26 August 2026 at the Globus Center in Baku. It aimed to let trainees discover different official roles and experience their working styles, and to develop soft skills such as transparent communication, ethical leadership and sustainable governance.

The training was also meant to help trainees decide which official streams they wanted to pursue in future sessions.
