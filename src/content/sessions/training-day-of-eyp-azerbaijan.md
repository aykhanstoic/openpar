The Training Day of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 11 April 2026 at the Globus Center. It was designed as a crash course on official positions for EYPers who had already taken part in sessions and were ready to take on more responsibility.

The day covered different official roles, their responsibilities and expectations, selection processes, and practical aspects of teamwork and communication within the teams. Its aim was to prepare motivated delegates to step into official positions with confidence, while promoting a transparent, value-driven and sustainable working culture within EYP Azerbaijan.
