The 12th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan will take place in Baku and will bring together around 200 young people from Azerbaijan and other European countries.

The programme will begin on 11 August 2026 with the CMOJ Day, the training day for officials. Delegates will join the session on 12 August for the Teambuilding Day, work in committees on 13 and 14 August, and debate at the General Assembly on 15 and 16 August. The session's theme is “All Roads Lead Home: The Politics of Passage”.
