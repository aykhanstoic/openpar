The 6th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku from 17 to 20 March 2018, during the national holiday of Novruz.

The session was planned to gather more than 120 delegates from across Europe and the districts of Azerbaijan to discuss inclusion and acceptance, including the recognition of cultural diversity and uniqueness. The five-day programme was planned to include a day of officials' training, a day of teambuilding, two days of committee work and a day of General Assembly.
