The 5th Jubilee National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 25-27 November 2016 in Baku, with the concept EYPLOVE and under the motto “Only Love can do it...”. The conference was dedicated to the recent terror attacks, civil war and other negative events.

Over the three days, participants discussed different topics in their committees, exchanged knowledge and shared experience.
