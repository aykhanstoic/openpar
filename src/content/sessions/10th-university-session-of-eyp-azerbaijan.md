The 10th University Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held from 17 to 20 June 2025 under the motto “Endless Growth, Timeless Harmony”. The motto was inspired by the Fibonacci spiral and the symbolism of natural balance.

The session aimed to celebrate continuous progress, unity and self-discovery, and to support participants' intellectual and personal growth, rooted in core values and driven by collaboration.
