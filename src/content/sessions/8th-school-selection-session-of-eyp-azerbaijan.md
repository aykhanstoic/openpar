The 8th School Selection Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 27-29 January 2023, with 26 January as the CMO training day. Under the theme “Self-realisation through Education”, it aimed to cultivate a deeper learning environment for participants and help them unravel their potential in EYP.

The most successful delegations, as well as some individual delegates, were to be chosen to attend the 9th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan, planned for March 2023.
