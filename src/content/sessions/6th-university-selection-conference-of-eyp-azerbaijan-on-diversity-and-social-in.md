The 6th University Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan on Diversity and Social Inclusion was held in Baku from 27 to 30 June 2019, with 26 June as the CJO day. Under the theme “Breaking Barriers, Building Bridges: Ensuring the Diversity and Inclusion”, it aimed to broaden the concept of “Active Engagement of European Youth” by advancing understanding of the organisation and its accessibility to anyone living in Europe, and welcomed participants from diverse social and economic backgrounds.

The conference was planned to gather more than 100 participants from across Europe to discuss topics on diversity and social inclusion, with one day of teambuilding, two days of committee work and one day of General Assembly. As a selection conference, it aimed to select delegates to take part in EYP events abroad, with travel reimbursement provided.
