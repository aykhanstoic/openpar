The 7th Summer Training Camp of EYP Azerbaijan was held from 11 to 13 July 2025 in Gobustan, under the motto “Bloom Through Challenges, Lead with Strength”. Inspired by the symbolism of the lotus flower, the camp was built on the idea that true strength and leadership emerge through overcoming challenges.

Set in nature and guided by the spirit of EYP, the camp aimed to give participants a space for personal and collective growth, with interactive learning, stronger essential skills and meaningful connections.
