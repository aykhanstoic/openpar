EYP Day in “ASAN Service” was held on 8 December 2013 in Baku. It was planned as a gathering of EYPers from across Azerbaijan to share their experience with one another.

The event was organised by EYP Azerbaijan together with the ASAN Volunteers Youth Union, with which EYP Azerbaijan had signed a memorandum of co-operation.
