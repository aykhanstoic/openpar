Model European Union was a joint project of EYP Azerbaijan, supported by the Delegation of the European Union to Azerbaijan. Its format differed from the standard EYP session: it was planned to include three meetings and negotiations and a final simulation day.

Participants were to be allocated to different institutions, such as the European Commission, the European Parliament, the Council of the EU and some stakeholders, and given particular roles. Each was to prepare a position paper on a given topic and legislative proposal, after first completing the Understanding Europe training.
