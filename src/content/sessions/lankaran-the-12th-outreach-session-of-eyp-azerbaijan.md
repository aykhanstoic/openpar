The 12th Outreach Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Lankaran on 23-24 August 2025, with 22 August as the CMO day. It took place under the motto “Honoring Roots: Infusing the Culture, Guiding the Youth”.

Its main aim was to create a safe and enjoyable environment for Lankaran's young people, while drawing on the city's nature and historical culture. The session's vision was to show respect for traditional appearance, history and culture, and to connect them with the generation of today.
