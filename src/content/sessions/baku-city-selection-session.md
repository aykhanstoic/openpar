The Baku City Selection Session was held on 2-4 February 2024 in Baku under the motto “Capture Today, Envision Tomorrow”. It aimed to encourage young people to pause and reflect on the present and on the future of their city, exploring their surroundings through the lens of the core values of the European Youth Parliament.

The session set the motto in the context of Baku preparing to host COP29 and of the Baku City General Plan 2040, which names the economy, environment and sustainability among its priorities. It also focused on active citizenship and on sharing ideas for positive change in the city.
