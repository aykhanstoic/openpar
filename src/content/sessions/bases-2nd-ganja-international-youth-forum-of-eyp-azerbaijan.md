The 2nd International Youth Forum of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Ganja from 18 to 22 August 2016, with officials arriving from 17 August. The session was planned to gather more than 120 participants from across Europe.

Ganja, the second largest city of Azerbaijan, held the title of European Youth Capital 2016, which was awarded to the city in 2013 by the European Youth Forum.
