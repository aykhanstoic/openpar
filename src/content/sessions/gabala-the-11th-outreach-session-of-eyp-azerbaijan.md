The 11th Gabala Outreach Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 26-27 April 2025 in Gabala, under the motto “Seeing the Unseen, Shaping the Unwritten”. The session was conducted entirely in the Azerbaijani language.

It brought together young people from the region and gave them a platform to discuss pressing global issues, exchange perspectives and foster civic engagement. Participants explored challenges such as climate change, the societal impact of technological advancement and social inequalities, and worked on solutions together.
