The Training Weekend of EYP Azerbaijan was a two-day, capacity-building event for existing members of EYP Azerbaijan, held in Baku on 13-14 April 2024. It took place under the motto “Expanding the horizons”.

It included modules in the Azerbaijani language, to reach more people and introduce EYP values to them.
