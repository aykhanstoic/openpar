The 7th University Session of EYP Azerbaijan took place in Baku from 15 to 19 April 2020, with 15 April as the CJO day. The session was planned to welcome participants from across Europe, who would debate a range of topics linked by the general theme of Civic Engagement, Altruism and Volunteerism.

The most successful delegations and some individual delegates were to be chosen to attend the 8th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan, planned for the summer of 2020.
