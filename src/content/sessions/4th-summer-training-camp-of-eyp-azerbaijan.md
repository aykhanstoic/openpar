The 4th Summer Training Camp of EYP Azerbaijan was held from 26 to 29 August 2021. It aimed to gather participants, including active alumni of EYP Azerbaijan and EYPers from across Europe, and to build their skills in areas such as chairing, organising, media and international governance.

The camp was intended as an alumni development and capacity-building event of EYP Azerbaijan, and as a learning experience for both experienced participants and people with limited EYP experience.
