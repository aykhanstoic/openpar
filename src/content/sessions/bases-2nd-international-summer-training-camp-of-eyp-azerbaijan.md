The 2nd Summer Training Camp of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Quba on 15-19 August 2018. It was an initiative to cultivate a culture of training in EYP Azerbaijan, and was intended to bring together active alumni of EYP Azerbaijan and EYPers from across Europe.

The camp aimed to strengthen the skills of EYPers needed for taking part in EYP events in three areas: chairing, organising and media. It was meant as an alumni development and capacity-building event, working towards more qualified officials at EYP events in Azerbaijan and abroad, and was open to both experienced participants and those with little EYP experience.
