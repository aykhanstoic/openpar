Guided by the motto “Rooted in Our Past. United in Our Present”, the 2nd Baku City Session of EYP Azerbaijan will take place from 24 to 27 December 2026. It aims to bring young people together to exchange ideas, develop their skills and discuss contemporary European challenges.

By connecting the lessons of the past with the opportunities of the present, the session will seek to foster collaboration, mutual understanding and a shared sense of responsibility for shaping the future.
