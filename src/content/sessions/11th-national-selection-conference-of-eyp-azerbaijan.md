The 11th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 8-12 August 2025 under the motto “Digital Skies, Boundless Horizons”. The session explored the future of aerospace and digital innovation, focusing on the intersection of technology, sustainability and connectivity.

It invited participants to reflect on how AI, satellite systems, eco-friendly aviation and interplanetary communication are reshaping the world, and on the role of young people in navigating digital transformation and building a more innovative, secure and interconnected future.
