The 5th School Session of the European Youth Parliament (EYP) Azerbaijan, the first session of the new term, was held on 17-18 November 2018 in Baku. A CJO training for officials was scheduled for 16 November.

The session was planned to gather over 100 young people from across Europe to discuss topics relevant to young people today.
