The 6th School Session was the first session of EYP Azerbaijan in 2020. It was held in Baku from 21 to 23 February 2020, with 20 February as the CJO day.

The session was planned to gather 120 participants from schools across Azerbaijan and beyond to discuss pressing issues concerning Europe.
