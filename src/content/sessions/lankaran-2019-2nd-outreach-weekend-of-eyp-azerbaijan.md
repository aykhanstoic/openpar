The 2nd Outreach Event of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 13-14 April 2019 in Lankaran. It brought together young people from Lankaran and Lerik, two regions of Azerbaijan, to broaden the reach of EYP and share the ideas of the EU. It was planned for 40 participants from each region, with debates and workshops on issues in Azerbaijan and the European Union.

The event aimed to give young people in less developed regions a chance to experience the European Youth Parliament and to develop themselves, and to introduce those interested to what EYP stands for, so that they might later join as active members.
