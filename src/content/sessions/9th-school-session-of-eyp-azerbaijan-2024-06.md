The 9th School Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 20-23 June 2024 in Baku under the theme “Igniting Passion, Lighting Excellence”. It aimed to inspire young people across Europe to tap into their inner strengths, make a meaningful difference and reach excellence in what they do, with an emphasis on ethical leadership and pursuing goals with integrity and dedication.

The programme was planned to include workshops in fields such as art and career planning, debates, and alumni sharing their career paths, opportunities and challenges.
