The Regional Session of EYP Azerbaijan on Multiculturalism was held in Baku from 25 to 27 March 2016. The session was dedicated to multiculturalism, as 2016 was the Year of Multiculturalism in Azerbaijan.

Delegates were planned to be allocated to six committees to discuss assigned topics within the scope of multiculturalism, exchanging attitudes and experiences and getting to know various perspectives.
