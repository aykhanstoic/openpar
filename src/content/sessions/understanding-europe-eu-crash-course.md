The Understanding Europe EU Crash Course was a four-hour seminar on the EU, its structure, institutions and policies, held in Baku on 18 December 2016. The working language was English.

Understanding Europe is a project run by the Schwarzkopf Foundation since 2009, which EYP Azerbaijan joined in early 2015. This was to be the 7th EU Crash Course in Azerbaijan.
