Theia, the 7th University Session of EYP Azerbaijan, was held in Baku from 26 to 28 November 2022, with 25 November as the CJO day.

The most successful delegations, as well as some individual delegates, were to be chosen to attend the 9th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan, planned for March 2023.
