GARABAGH'18, the 3rd International Forum of EYP Azerbaijan, was held on 3-7 July 2018 in Barda, with a CJO training in Baku on 2 July. The session was supported by Qarabagh FC and named after the club.

The session was dedicated to humanness, focusing on the importance of humanity, empathy and kindness in everyday life. Participants were to discuss a range of topics within their committees and to exchange knowledge and experience.
