The 5th University Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku in February 2019, with the session dates including the CJO day running from 14 to 17 February.

The session aimed to bring together participants from National Committees across Europe, planned at more than 120, to discuss and problematise various topics within their committees and to share knowledge and experience.
