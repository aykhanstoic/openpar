The Baku City Session of EYP Azerbaijan on Unity and Broadening Horizons was held on 8-11 August 2019, with 8 August as the CJO day. The session was conducted entirely in the Azerbaijani language.

As a continuation of the Outreach events, it aimed to make EYP more accessible to future EYPers by removing the language barrier. Its topic was unification: that, however different people are, coming together helps them grow and broaden their view and understanding of the world.
