The Baku City Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held for the 2nd time on 7-9 March 2020 in Baku. It served the purposes of the national strategy of EYP Azerbaijan on Outreach and Inclusion, and aimed to provide equal opportunities to everyone willing to participate.

The session was planned to gather over 70 local students from the regions of Azerbaijan and the suburbs of Baku. Azerbaijani was the official session language, so that students with little or no English could take part.
