The National Safety Training of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 28 June 2025. It aimed to strengthen participants' ability to foster safe, respectful and inclusive environments across all EYP activities.

Through interactive, practice-based training, it looked at how to respond to challenging situations, how to define individual responsibilities, and how to promote a culture of care and accountability within the network.
