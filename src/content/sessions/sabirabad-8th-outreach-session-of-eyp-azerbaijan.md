The Sabirabad Outreach Session was held on 21 April 2024, with a CMO day on 20 April. The session was in the Azerbaijani language. It aimed to promote EYP values such as diversity, intercultural learning and active citizenship, while having a lasting effect on the regions of Azerbaijan.

The session was planned as a one-day event for local young people from Sabirabad.
