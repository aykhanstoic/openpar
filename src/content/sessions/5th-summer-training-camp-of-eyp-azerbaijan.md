The 5th Summer Training Camp of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 10-13 August 2022, with 9 August as the CMO day. It was planned to take place in one of the regions of Azerbaijan.

The camp aimed to bring together trainees from Azerbaijan and across Europe to learn more about how EYP works and to prepare to become the next generation of officials.
