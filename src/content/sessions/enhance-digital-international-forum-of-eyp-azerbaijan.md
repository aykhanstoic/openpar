Enhance, the Digital International Forum of EYP Azerbaijan, was held on 17-20 June 2021, with the officials' training on 16 June. It was supported by the Visegrad Fund.

The session invited participants to learn and think about the diversity and plurality among European countries and the individuals within them, and aimed to be a space for understanding the issues of Europe and the world today and how they connect. It also aimed to help participants develop skills, tools and confidence to make an impact, with an emphasis on sharing knowledge and on inclusion, accessibility and exchange.
