The Ganja Regional Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 6-8 December 2024 in Ganja, a city known for its natural landscapes, the nearby Goygol National Park and its connection to Azerbaijan's cultural heritage. Under the theme “Green Horizons: Youth and Nature in Harmony”, it called on young people in Azerbaijan to take an active role in environmental leadership and in preserving the natural environment.

The session was to include discussions on eco-friendly innovation and sustainable agriculture, and debates on renewable energy solutions, fields relevant to Ganja's agricultural roots. It aimed to show local young people how nature can drive both personal growth and sustainable community development.
