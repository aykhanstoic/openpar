The Ganja City Session was held on 26-28 November 2021 as an event with the full EYP experience for participants from Ganja, the third-largest city of Azerbaijan, about 300 km from Baku. It was planned for 60 delegates from Ganja, who were to be introduced to the main EYP concepts through teambuilding, committee work and a General Assembly.

The event was also intended to serve as a base for establishing a permanent representation of EYP Azerbaijan in Ganja. It was supported by the International Office within the framework of the BASES project.
