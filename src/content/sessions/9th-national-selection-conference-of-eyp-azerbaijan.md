The 9th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku from 21 to 26 March 2023, with the sessions running from 22 to 26 March and 21 March as the CMOJ day. It was planned to gather more than 200 participants from Azerbaijan and across Europe, and the delegates chosen by the Jury Team were to represent EYP Azerbaijan in the Baku 2023 and Istanbul 2024 International Sessions.

Under the theme “Unity in Diversity: Embrace, Share, Celebrate”, the conference encouraged academic discussions and debates on art and culture as a tool for uniting people, enhancing harmony through diversity and guiding people to enlightenment.
