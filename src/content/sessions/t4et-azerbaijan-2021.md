T4ET Azerbaijan 2021, a Training for EYP Trainers, was held on 4-10 October 2021 in Azerbaijan. Trainings of this kind contribute to the development of the organisation and to the growth of its active members, and aim to raise the quality of events and strengthen EYP as a training-based organisation.

The aim of T4ET was to enable participants to become confident, competent, experienced trainers, ready to deliver trainings across the network and pass on their experience. The 2021 training was supported by the Federal Foreign Office within the Civil Society Cooperation project “Youth Dialogue for Strong Civic Spaces”.
