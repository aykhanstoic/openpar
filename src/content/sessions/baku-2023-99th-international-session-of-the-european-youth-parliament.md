The 99th International Session of the European Youth Parliament was held in Baku, the capital of Azerbaijan, from 2 to 13 August 2023. It was the first International Session hosted in Baku.

The organisers, drawn from different generations of EYP Azerbaijan, chose a theme on shaping an education system that meets the needs and demands of a fast-changing world. The session aimed to give young people a stronger voice in building such a system.
