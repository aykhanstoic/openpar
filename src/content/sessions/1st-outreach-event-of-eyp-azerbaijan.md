The 1st Outreach Event of EYP Azerbaijan was held on 15-16 December 2018 in Quba. It brought together young people from Quba and Qusar, two regions of Azerbaijan, to broaden the reach of EYP and share the ideas of the EU. It was planned for 40 participants from each region, with debates and workshops on issues in Azerbaijan and the European Union.

The event aimed to give young people in less developed regions a chance to experience the European Youth Parliament and to develop themselves, and to introduce those interested to what EYP stands for, so that they might later join as active members.
