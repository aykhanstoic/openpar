Genesis, the 8th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan, was held in person in Baku on 24-27 March 2022, with 23 March as the CJO day. The session aimed to gather 100 delegates from Azerbaijan and across Europe to debate topics openly and find solutions that could be implemented in the near future.

Under the theme “Embracing the past for a better future”, Genesis was intended as a session for everyone to unlock their potential. Delegates of EYP Azerbaijan selected at the session by the jury were to have the chance to take part in the 96th and 97th International Sessions of EYP.
