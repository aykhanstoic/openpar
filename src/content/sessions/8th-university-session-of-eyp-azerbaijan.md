The 8th University Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku from 26 to 29 May 2023, with 26 May as the CMO training day.

Under the slogan “Youth Involvement: Encouraging Growth, Fostering Collaboration”, the organisers aimed to make the session a place for new meaningful connections and further involvement with the organisation. It was planned to bring together 160 participants.
