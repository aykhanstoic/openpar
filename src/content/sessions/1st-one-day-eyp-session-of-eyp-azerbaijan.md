The 1st One Day EYP Session of EYP Azerbaijan, on the topic of Modern Globalisation, was held at Istek Lyceum in Baku on 30 October 2016. The session was planned to bring together approximately 65 students to discuss topics related to Modern Globalisation within their committees and to introduce them to the structure of political debates.
