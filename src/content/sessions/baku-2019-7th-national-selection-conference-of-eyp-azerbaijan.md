The 7th National Selection Conference of EYP Azerbaijan was held in Baku from 20 to 24 March 2019, with 20 March as the CJO day, during the Novruz national holiday. Participants discussed topics on Energy Governance. The conference was supported by Innogy Foundation.

The programme was planned to include one day of officials' training, one day of teambuilding, two days of committee work and one day of General Assembly.
