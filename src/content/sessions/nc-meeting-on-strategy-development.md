The NC Meeting on Strategy Development was held on Sunday 4 August 2024 at 16:00 (Baku time), in a hybrid format with an in-person location in Baku and online attendance. It was intended for National Committee members, department members, alumni and active members of EYP Azerbaijan.

The meeting was fully discussion-based and gave members an opportunity to share insights, opinions, feedback and suggestions on the strategic development of EYP Azerbaijan. The agenda covered strategies for:

- capacity building
- delegate engagement
- enhancing participation quality and experience from diverse backgrounds
- increasing application and membership interest
- other organisational challenges and opportunities
