The 3rd Summer Training Camp of EYP Azerbaijan was held from 15 to 18 August 2019 in Gabala, in the north of Azerbaijan. It was planned to gather up to 120 participants, including active alumni of EYP Azerbaijan and EYPers from across Europe.

The main aim was to strengthen the skills of young EYPers in areas such as Chairing, Organising, Media and International Governance. The camp was meant as an Alumni Development and Capacity Building event, and as a learning experience for both experienced participants and those with little EYP experience.
