The 10th School Session of EYP Azerbaijan was held from 27 to 29 March 2025, during the Novruz holiday, under the motto “Flames of Renewal: Igniting Unity Across Cultures”. Drawing inspiration from the symbolism of fire and nature, the session aimed to ignite enthusiasm and nurture fresh ideas, bringing young people together to explore sustainability, cultural preservation and community-driven progress.

It also aimed to foster leadership, cooperation and resilience, and to emphasise honouring traditions while shaping a united future through collaboration and innovation.
