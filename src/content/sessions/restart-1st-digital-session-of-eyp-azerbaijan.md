Restart, the 1st Digital Session of EYP Azerbaijan, was held online on 9-12 July 2020. It was the first fully digital session of EYP Azerbaijan, organised in response to the lockdown and closure of borders across Europe.

The session was inspired by an investigation of the digital transformation of what was once everyday life. It aimed to show how digital connections are creating a “new normal” and giving the world an increased global outlook and digital presence.
