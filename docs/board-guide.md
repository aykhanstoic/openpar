# Editing the website

You change the website at **/admin** (for example `https://eyp.az/admin/`). You do not need an
account, only the shared password.

## Log in

Open `/admin` and enter the password. Ask whoever manages the website if you do not have it.

## What you can change

- **Page text**: the wording of every page, English and Azerbaijani side by side. Fill in both
  languages.
- **Sessions, news, board members, partners**: add, edit or delete.
- **Page photos** (`/admin/photos`): the photos on the Home, About us and Get involved pages.
  Each photo needs a short description in both languages.
- **Menu, footer and labels**: under the page text.

## Saving

1. Make your change and write **your name** in the "Your name" box. It is kept in the history so
   we can see who changed what.
2. Press Save. If something is wrong, you see "Nothing was saved" and the boxes to fix are marked
   in red. Your photo stays selected.
3. After a successful save, **the website updates about two minutes later**. Refresh then.

## Photos

- Use JPG, PNG or WebP. iPhones may save photos as HEIC, which is not accepted: set
  Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or share the photo as JPG.
- Large photos are shrunk for you. Phone photos are fine.
- Photos are cleaned of hidden data such as the location where they were taken.
- Use a wide (landscape) photo for the news cover. Only upload photos of people we have
  permission to publish, and for under-18s that means their parents' permission.

## Deleting

Open the entry, scroll to "Delete", tick the box, write your name and press the button. A
session that a news post links to cannot be deleted: the message names the posts. Remove the
"Related session" in those posts first.

## If something goes wrong

Do not try again and again. Write down what you pressed and what the message said, and send it
to whoever manages the website. Nothing is lost: every save is kept in the history.

## Password

Do not share it outside the board. To remove someone's access, the password is changed and
everyone logs in again with the new one.
